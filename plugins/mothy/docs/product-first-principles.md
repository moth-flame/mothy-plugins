## Ideation and Design

- **Envision solutions to customers' problems and work backward.** Customer convenience
  beats our convenience.
- **Think big, start small.** Gall's law: complex systems that work evolve from simple
  systems that worked. Simple scales, fancy fails.
- **Be stubborn on the vision and flexible on the details.** Preserve optionality while
  executing.
- **Balance versioning with visioning.** Incremental improvement AND longer-term bets.
- **Be clear, not clever.** In UI, in copy, in documents.
- **Build for customers, not clients.** No custom one-offs that don't scale to the market.
- **Be opinionated out of the box but flexible under the hood.**
- **Prototype the promise before you write the promise.** The demo comes before the
  document.
- **Product ideas from anywhere; product decisions from evidence.** Anyone can frame a
  problem and prototype; nobody gets build resources without validated evidence.

## Planning and Execution

- **Don't shop without a price tag.** Know the complexity and tradeoffs before committing.
- **Don't take shortcuts.** No empty-calorie work; no technical debt dumped on future us.
- **Make reversible decisions quickly and irreversible decisions carefully.** 2-way doors:
  walk through; 1-way doors: align stakeholders first.
- **Ship to learn.** Bullets before cannonballs — calibrate with small, quick shots.
- **Measure what we value, and value what we measure.** Instrument for learning BEFORE
  shipping; the North Star metric measures itself.
- **Practice Extreme Ownership.** A mistake made more than once is a decision.
- **Fish or cut bait.** Kill languishing features; date the decision.
- **Spend tokens before engineer-weeks.** Buy answers with AI-built prototypes, evals, and
  drafts first; human build attention is for what earned it.
- **Cheap experiments turn one-way doors into two-way doors.** Reserve the slow path for
  doors that are still truly one-way.
